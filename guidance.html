Beginner guidance

Simple rituals, used thoughtfully.

Glitter Witch products are intended to support intention-setting, reflection and personal spiritual practice. The meaning comes from the care and attention you bring to the ritual.

Prepare your space

Choose a quiet, stable and well-ventilated place. Read every instruction before beginning and keep the area clear of anything flammable.

Choose your intention

Decide what you wish to reflect on, welcome, release or strengthen. Keep the intention realistic, personal and positively worded.

Close with care

Finish slowly, extinguish candles safely, store all items responsibly and take a moment to note any thoughts or feelings that arose.

Candle safety matters

Never leave a burning candle unattended. Keep candles away from children, pets, draughts, curtains, paper, dried herbs and other flammable items. Use a stable, heat-resistant holder and extinguish the flame before leaving the room or going to sleep.

Products should always include their own specific safe-use instructions. Those instructions take priority over general guidance on this website.

A clear and honest promise

Glitter Witch ritual products are not medical, legal, financial or mental-health treatments. They are reflective and spiritual products and do not guarantee a particular result. Professional support should be sought whenever it is needed.
