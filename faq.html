Questions and answers

Everything made clear.

This page can grow as real customer questions begin to arrive.

Are Glitter Witch books suitable for beginners?

Yes. The collection was written as a gentle introduction for readers who are new to witchcraft, spells, tarot, candle work and simple daily ritual practice.

Where are the books sold?

The books are sold through Amazon. The Books page links directly to the supplied Amazon listings. Amazon handles payment, printing, delivery and Kindle access.

What will be inside a ritual box?

Every product page will list the exact contents. Depending on the collection, a box may include a candle, spell bag, instruction card, affirmation or reflection card, crystal, charm or other clearly described items.

Will products promise that a spell will definitely work?

No. The products are intended for reflection, intention-setting and spiritual practice. They will not make guaranteed supernatural, medical, financial or relationship claims.

Who manages the products and inventory?

Carol will manage product photographs, descriptions, prices and inventory in Shopify. Noel will manage the custom website, GitHub, the Shopify connection and the domain.

Can a customer buy more than one product?

Yes. Once Shopify is connected, products can be added to a Shopify-powered basket before the customer moves to secure checkout.

What happens when an item sells out?

Shopify will control inventory. When tracked stock reaches zero, the product can be shown as unavailable unless selling past zero has been deliberately enabled.

Will the website work on phones?

Yes. The website has been designed to adjust for desktop computers, tablets and mobile phones.
