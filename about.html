A gentle path into everyday magic

Carol writes under the pen name Glitter Witch. Her books grew from a desire to explain spiritual subjects in a way that feels kind, practical and easy to follow, especially for readers who are only beginning their journey.

For some people, witchcraft is a spiritual path. For others, it is a way of connecting with nature, listening to intuition or bringing more meaning and intention into ordinary life. Carol’s writing respects those different starting points.

No unusual abilities, expensive tools or years of experience are required. The books invite readers to begin with curiosity, patience, respect and an open heart.

Magic does not have to be complicated to feel meaningful.
