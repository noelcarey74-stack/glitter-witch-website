Carol Carey is Glitter Witch
Practising witch · Author · Guide

A genuine spiritual practice shared with care

Glitter Witch is Carol Carey. It is the name under which she shares her books, knowledge, rituals and personal approach to witchcraft. Everything within the Glitter Witch Collection grows from Carol’s own life as a practising witch and from her connection with nature, intuition, ritual and everyday spiritual practice.

Carol believes that witchcraft does not need to feel frightening, secretive or complicated. It can be a gentle and positive way of creating time for reflection, listening to intuition and bringing greater meaning and purpose into ordinary life.

Through her books, Carol shares what she has learned in a warm, understandable and beginner-friendly way. Her aim is to help readers begin their own journey with curiosity, kindness, confidence, patience and respect.

Magic does not have to be complicated to feel meaningful.

From books to ritual collections

Created with meaning and honest guidance

The Glitter Witch ritual shop will follow the same principles as Carol’s books. Every ritual box, spell bag and candle collection will be clearly described, beautifully presented and accompanied by practical, beginner-friendly guidance.

Carol’s products are intended to support reflection, intention-setting and personal spiritual practice. They will not be presented as guaranteed solutions or as replacements for professional medical, legal, financial or mental-health support.

Their purpose is to help people create a meaningful moment, connect with their own intentions and bring a little more calm, confidence and everyday magic into their lives.
