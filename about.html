Meet the woman behind Glitter Witch

Meet Carol Carey

Carol is a practising witch, author and creator of The Glitter Witch Collection. Her work is inspired by nature, intuition, positive spellwork and the belief that everyday magic can be meaningful, personal and accessible.

Carol Carey · Glitter Witch
Practising witch · Author · Creator

A genuine spiritual practice shared with care

Glitter Witch is more than an author name or a fictional character. It grew from Carol’s own life as a practising witch and from her personal connection with nature, intuition, ritual and everyday spiritual practice.

Carol believes that witchcraft does not need to feel frightening, secretive or complicated. It can be a gentle and positive way of creating time for reflection, understanding personal intuition and bringing greater purpose into ordinary life.

Through her books, Carol shares the knowledge and experience she has gathered in a warm, understandable and beginner-friendly way. Her aim is to help readers begin their own journey with curiosity, kindness, confidence and respect.

Magic does not have to be complicated to feel meaningful.

From books to ritual collections

Created with meaning and honest guidance

The Glitter Witch ritual shop will follow the same principles as Carol’s books. Every ritual box, spell bag and candle collection will be clearly described, beautifully presented and accompanied by practical guidance.

Carol’s products are intended to support reflection, intention-setting and personal spiritual practice. They are not presented as guaranteed solutions or replacements for professional medical, legal, financial or mental-health support.

The purpose is to help people create a meaningful moment, connect with their own intention and bring a little more calm, confidence and magic into everyday life.
