Meet the author

Carol Carey writes as Glitter Witch.

Carol created the Glitter Witch books to make spiritual subjects feel calm, encouraging and easy to understand. Her writing welcomes complete beginners while giving experienced readers practical ideas they can return to.

Every title is part of one growing creative world—beautifully presented, thoughtfully written and rooted in curiosity, intention and everyday practice.
