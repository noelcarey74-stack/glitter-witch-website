Meet the author

Carol Carey writes as Glitter Witch.

Carol created the Glitter Witch books especially for complete beginners. Her aim is to make spiritual learning feel calm, encouraging and easy to understand, without losing the sense of wonder that first draws people to it.

Her six-volume Introduction Series guides readers through witchcraft, spells, tarot, candle magic, positive spellwork and simple daily rituals. Every title is part of Carol’s own growing collection.

The heart of Glitter Witch

Magic made gentle, beautiful and approachable.

Glitter Witch was created for complete beginners and for anyone who feels drawn to nature, intuition and meaningful everyday practice. Nothing needs to be complicated, expensive or intimidating.

Every day is a chance to create your magic.

The books and ritual collections are intended to support reflection, intention-setting and personal spiritual practice. They do not promise guaranteed outcomes; the value is in the meaning you bring to the moment.
