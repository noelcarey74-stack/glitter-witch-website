Beginner guidance

Simple rituals, used thoughtfully.

Glitter Witch books and products support intention-setting, reflection and personal spiritual practice. The meaning comes from the attention you bring to the moment.

1

Prepare your space

Choose a calm place, gather what you need and read the full working before beginning.

2

Choose your intention

Decide what you wish to welcome, release, understand or strengthen. Keep the intention clear and personal.

3

Close with care

Finish slowly, put tools away and record anything that felt useful, symbolic or meaningful.

Let your practice become your own

Traditional correspondences provide a starting point, but your personal experiences will shape the meanings that become most useful to you.

You may adapt materials, shorten a ritual or return to one working many times. Consistency and personal connection often matter more than complexity.

Using candles

Use a stable holder, keep the flame clear of anything flammable and extinguish it before leaving the room. Flameless candles may be used whenever they suit the space better.
