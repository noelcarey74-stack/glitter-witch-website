Questions and answers

Everything made clear.

Answers about the books, the two collections and the ritual shop opening on 24 August 2026.

Are Glitter Witch books suitable for beginners?

Yes. The collections are written in a warm, practical style. Readers can begin with the first full-length volume or choose any pocket book that matches their interest.

How many Glitter Witch books are available?

There are fourteen published titles: eight full-length books in The Glitter Witch Collection and six books in The Glitter Witch Pocket Spell Book Collection.

Where are the books sold?

The books are sold through Amazon. Each book on the Books page has a paperback button for Amazon Ireland and a Kindle button for Amazon UK.

When does the Glitter Witch shop open?

The online ritual shop is planned to open on 24 August 2026.

What products will be available?

The planned range includes ritual boxes, spell bags, candles, crystal and altar pieces, gifts and Glitter Witch merchandise. Each listing will show the exact contents and availability.

How will shop payments work?

Available products will use secure Stripe checkout. Stripe processes payment details; card information is not stored by this website.

What happens when an item sells out?

The product will be marked unavailable until Carol prepares more stock. Availability will be shown on the shop page.

Will the website work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The website is responsive and has been designed for desktop computers, tablets and mobile phones.

Still have a question?

Carol can help with questions about books, products and the Glitter Witch website.
