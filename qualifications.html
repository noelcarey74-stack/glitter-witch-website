Qualifications & Training

Learning is a lifelong journey.

Alongside writing the Glitter Witch Collection, Carol has continued to study a wide range of spiritual and reflective subjects. These courses have helped shape the clear, practical and beginner-friendly approach found throughout her books.

Study · Practice · Growth

Committed to continued learning

Carol’s studies cover areas including witchcraft, tarot, candle magic, meditation, pendulum work, aura reading, spellcraft, white magic and daily spiritual practice.

The certificates below record completed courses and form part of Carol’s wider learning journey. They are presented here openly so readers can see the subjects she has explored while developing the Glitter Witch books and future ritual collections.

Every book grows from curiosity, study and a genuine desire to make learning feel welcoming.
